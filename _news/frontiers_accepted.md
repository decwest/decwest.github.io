廊下幅の異なる環境におけるロボットの走行経路の快適性を，搭乗者と観察者の両方の視点から評価した論文が **Frontiers in Robotics and AI** に採録されました！

“Evaluating the Comfort of Robot Paths in Varying-Width Corridors: Passenger vs. Observer Perspectives” has been accepted and published in **Frontiers in Robotics and AI**.

- [Published version (Open Access, DOI: 10.3389/frobt.2026.1971439)](https://doi.org/10.3389/frobt.2026.1971439)
